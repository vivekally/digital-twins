# Digital Twins

A six-slide dark-theme deck on the AI "digital twin of a person" category: what the
market actually is, who is funded, how the systems are built, what they are actually
sold to do, who signs the cheque, and how the whole thing relates to a company brain.

**Live:** https://vivekally.github.io/digital-twins/

## The slides

| | |
|---|---|
| **01 · Market** | Three different businesses are sharing one name. Only two of them have revenue anyone measures. |
| **02 · Players & capital** | Every disclosed round through Aug 2026, grouped by layer. The person is the least-funded layer of the person business. |
| **03 · Architecture** | Viven, Delphi and Tavus deconstructed into seven layers. Nobody is training a model of you. |
| **04 · Employee use cases** | Viven sells six use cases. Two have a reference deployment, one has a first-party claim, three have neither. Every vendor here converges on the same primitive. |
| **05 · The buyer** | Everyone files this under HR. Nobody sells it there. The buying centre is the CEO's office with IT as approver, and legal as the blocker. |
| **06 · Twins vs company brain** | Same machine, different unit of indexing. Around 80% of the build is identical, and the 20% that differs is consent. |

## Reading it

Arrow keys, space, or `Home` / `End` to move. Number keys `1`–`6` jump to a slide.
`S` opens the sources panel, `Esc` closes it. Swipe on touch.

Each slide scales itself to fit one screen, down to a readability floor, below which
it scrolls instead. `index.html` opens straight off disk, with no server and no build
step. Printing gives one page per slide.

## What is in here

```
index.html    the deck, self-contained: no framework, no build, no network calls
research/     the four source memos the deck was built from
```

The deck goes beyond the memos in `research/`. Funding, ARR, market sizing and the
regulatory material were re-verified against live sources in August 2026, and the
company-brain comparison on slide 06 is new. Slides 04 and 05 were re-validated in
September 2026 against viven.ai, the 15 Oct 2025 launch release and the live job
board. Sources are listed in the deck itself.

## Confidence

Funding and ARR figures come from primary announcements and are cited. Market-size and
CAGR figures are vendor-commissioned research that disagrees with itself by up to 3×,
and the deck says so on the slide rather than in a footnote. Viven's architecture is
described from a single founder interview and is labelled claimed, not verified.

Slides 04 and 05 rest almost entirely on first-party evidence, which shows marketed
positioning rather than proven behaviour, and both say so on the slide. Two known
weak points are called out in place: the WSJ figures circulating in this category
belong to Reid Hoffman and Greif rather than to Viven, and the round lead is reported
both ways. One figure carried on slide 01, the Gartner "95% / 80%" HR-adoption stat,
could not be re-verified at source in September 2026 and is flagged here rather than
silently repeated.
