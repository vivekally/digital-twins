Key signals from the scorecard

The incumbents score highest overall. Microsoft Copilot Memory leads at 7.9 because it ships across every dimension with the deepest integration surface. That is the finding: the buyer pays for breadth and trust, not depth of persona fidelity.

Viven and Delphi tie at 7.3 among pure-play twins. They lead on different axes: Viven on privacy, governance and enterprise deployment; Delphi on grounding, citations and embodiment. Neither has published a latency benchmark or revenue figure.

No product scores above 8 overall. The category is nascent. Independent verification is missing across the board. Treat every score as directional, not definitive.

Dimensions

15 dimensions. Each evaluates one capability that matters when an organization deploys a digital twin of a specific employee or expert.

01
Knowledge Capture
Test: Feed 50+ documents and meeting transcripts, then quiz the twin on obscure details only the original person would know.
How completely and accurately the twin ingests, structures, and retains a person's knowledge corpus. Includes depth of understanding, not just keyword retrieval.
02
Response Fidelity
Test: Compare the twin's answers to the real person's answers on 10 domain questions. Score alignment of voice, reasoning, and specificity.
How closely the twin reproduces the original person's communication style, expertise, judgment, and decision-making patterns. Not just facts — the how, not just the what.
03
Source Integrations
Test: Connect to 5 enterprise tools (email, chat, docs, calendar, CRM). Count working out-of-the-box connectors.
Breadth and depth of data source connectors. Gmail, Slack, Teams, Drive, SharePoint, Notion, Jira, Confluence, Salesforce, meeting transcripts, and more.
04
Privacy & Governance
Test: Set access rules restricting who can query what. Verify enforcement. Check for audit trails, encryption, compliance certifications.
RBAC, audit logging, data encryption at rest and in transit, SOC 2 / ISO 27001, GDPR / CCPA compliance, data residency controls, and consent management.
05
Deployment Options
Test: Attempt to deploy as cloud SaaS, on-premises, hybrid, and BYOC (bring your own cloud). Score flexibility.
Where and how the product can run. Cloud-only SaaS, private cloud, on-premises, hybrid, VPC peering, air-gapped, and bring-your-own-cloud configurations.
06
Voice & Avatar
Test: Generate a voice response and a video response. Score naturalness, likeness, and latency.
Text-only vs. voice cloning vs. full video avatar. Quality of voice synthesis, lip-sync accuracy, real-time conversation capability, and visual fidelity.
07
Grounding & Citations
Test: Ask 10 questions requiring sourced answers. Score citation accuracy, hallucination rate, and "I don't know" calibration.
Whether answers cite their sources, link back to original documents, and refuse to answer when knowledge is absent. The inverse of hallucination.
08
Setup Speed
Test: Time from zero to first working twin. Include data ingestion, configuration, and initial query readiness.
How quickly a new twin becomes operational. Minutes (self-serve), hours (guided), days (enterprise deployment), or weeks (custom integration).
09
Query Latency
Test: Measure time-to-first-token and full response time for 10 knowledge queries of varying complexity.
Speed of the twin's responses. Sub-second for simple retrieval, under 5 seconds for complex reasoning. Includes infrastructure performance claims.
10
Team & Org Features
Test: Set up a team twin spanning 3 people. Query the team as a unit. Test admin console, user management, and cross-querying.
Org-wide capabilities: team twins, cross-employee querying, admin console, user provisioning, department-level views, and organizational knowledge graphs.
11
API & Extensibility
Test: Build a custom integration using the published API or SDK. Score documentation quality, endpoint coverage, and developer experience.
Developer access: REST/GraphQL APIs, SDKs, webhooks, embeddable widgets, and documentation. The ability to build on top of the twin.
12
Knowledge Freshness
Test: Add new data (a Slack message, a document edit). Query for it. Measure sync delay.
How quickly new information becomes queryable. Real-time streaming, hourly batch, daily reindex, or manual trigger. Includes change detection and incremental updates.
13
Access Control Granularity
Test: Set per-person, per-topic, per-document access rules. Verify the twin respects them when queried by different users.
Fine-grained authorization: pairwise privacy (twin answers differently based on who asks), topic-level restrictions, document-level ACLs, and temporal access windows.
14
Multi-language
Test: Query the twin in 3 non-English languages. Score response quality, cultural nuance, and consistency with English answers.
Language support breadth and quality. Number of supported languages, quality of non-English responses, cross-lingual retrieval, and culturally appropriate communication.
15
Pricing Accessibility
Test: Evaluate published pricing, free tiers, pilot programs, and total cost of ownership for a 50-person deployment.
Transparency and accessibility of pricing. Published pricing vs. "contact sales." Per-user, per-twin, or per-query models. Free tiers, trials, and pilot programs.

How to read scores

10 best-in-class 8 strong 6 adequate 4 weak 2 poor — not tested N/A doesn't apply

Overall is the mean of scored dimensions only. Untested (—) and N/A dimensions are excluded from the mean. A product with 5/15 tested and a 9.0 overall is not "better" than one with 15/15 tested and a 7.0 — the tested fraction matters.

How scoring works

One scale, open criteria, vendor-reported data. Independent testing is the goal; this version is a starting framework.

Scale

Every dimension is scored 1–10 after evaluating the product against published criteria. Scores are assigned based on public documentation, product demos, engineering blogs, customer case studies, and press coverage. No score comes from private access or paid testing.

9–10 Best-in-class. Sets the standard for the category.
7–8 Strong. Competitive, with clear evidence of capability.
5–6 Adequate. Functional but with notable gaps or missing evidence.
3–4 Weak. Present but limited. Significant improvement needed.
1–2 Poor. Barely functional or fundamentally missing.

Overall calculation

Overall = mean of all scored dimensions. Untested (—) and N/A dimensions are excluded. This means a product with fewer tested dimensions may show a higher overall if its tested areas are strong. Always read Overall alongside the Tested fraction.

A product scoring 7.5 on 13/15 dimensions tells you more than one scoring 8.5 on 3/15.

Category definitions

Enterprise / Employee twins: Products whose primary use case is replicating an employee's knowledge for organizational use — onboarding, knowledge continuity, cross-team querying.

Creator / Expert twins: Products that clone a creator, coach, or expert's knowledge and personality for audience engagement, lead generation, or monetization.

Adjacent: Infrastructure or simulation products that overlap with person-replica use cases but are not primarily about replicating a named individual's knowledge.

Incumbents: Platform companies whose products absorb digital-twin capabilities through personalization, memory, or knowledge-graph features.

Data sources & caveats

Scores are derived from: company websites, product documentation, engineering blogs, press releases, analyst coverage (Gartner, Forrester), funding announcements, customer case studies, and live product demos where accessible.

Key caveats:

• All data is vendor-reported unless noted. No independent lab testing has been performed.

• Funding totals and HQ details occasionally conflict across aggregators (Tracxn, Crunchbase, Dealroom).

• "Not tested" (—) means insufficient data to score, not that the capability is absent.

• Scores will change as products ship new features and as independent verification becomes available.

• The benchmark does not test for ethical considerations (consent, digital likeness rights, post-employment persistence) — those are policy questions, not product capabilities.
